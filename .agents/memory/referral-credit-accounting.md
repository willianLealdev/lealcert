---
name: Referral credit accounting
description: Financial and commercial treatment of certificates paid with referral credits.
---

Orders paid through referral credits must show the amount used in the order summary and must not count as cash revenue, seller earnings, seller bonus volume, or loyalty accumulation.

**Why:** Referral credits are an accumulated balance redemption, not a new financial payment to the business.

**How to apply:** When payment method is `credito_indicacao`, preserve the original order total for product auditability, record the credit consumed separately, and exclude the order from financial and commission aggregations. Keep operational order visibility intact.
